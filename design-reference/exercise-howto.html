Bench Press

Chest Triceps Front delts
SETS × REPS
3 × 8–12
REST
60s
EQUIPMENT
Barbell
LEVEL
Intermediate
How to
1
Lie flat on the bench, feet planted, and grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
2
Unrack and lower the bar under control to mid-chest.
3
Press up until your arms are extended, keeping your shoulders back.
4
Pause briefly at the top, then repeat for your reps.
Form tips
Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows.
Drive through your mid-back, not just your shoulders.
Don't bounce the bar off your chest.
